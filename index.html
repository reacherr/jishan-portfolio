01 On screen

Pop Kaun?

His first Bollywood credit. He wrote and composed the song for Farhad Samji’s Hotstar Specials comedy, with a cast of India’s best-loved comic actors.

Farhad Samji described the scene to him over a call. He wrote and composed the song in about two hours, sent it across as a voice note, and forgot about it until someone rang two months later asking for his KYC details so they could pay him. That was how he found out he was in the show.
